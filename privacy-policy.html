Blocktide Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

This policy explains how the Blocktide mobile game ("the game") handles information. The game is published by an independent developer ("we").

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress on your own device: your coins, best result, unlocked themes, chosen theme, sound setting, Rotate count and daily reward streak. We do not collect this information, and it is never sent to us. It is removed if you uninstall the game.

We do not ask for personal details

The game has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.

Advertising

The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob, a service provided by Google. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and use information from your device, including:

  • your device's advertising ID
  • your IP address and approximate location based on it
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system and language
  • information about how you interact with ads

This information is collected by Google, not by us. You can read how Google uses it at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your choices

If you are in a region where consent is required, such as the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, the game asks for your choice before personalised ads are shown.

On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings, under Privacy, then Ads. You can also control personalised ads at adssettings.google.com.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will appear on this page with a new date.
